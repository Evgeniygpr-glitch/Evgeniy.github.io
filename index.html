About

I am an independent developer and researcher working on software and hardware projects related to geospatial technologies, historical research and archaeological exploration.

My interests include archaeology, local history, historical maps, remote sensing, geographic information systems and the use of modern technologies for studying the past.

I also develop experimental devices and electronic systems for detecting and investigating underground objects.

Projects

GeoPredict

GeoPredict is an independent Android application project focused on geographic and historical data analysis.

The project explores the use of terrain data, satellite imagery, historical maps and historical sources to help identify areas that may be of interest for further field research.

Experimental Hardware

Development and experimentation with electronic devices for detecting and investigating underground objects, including pulse-based sensing and signal-processing systems.
